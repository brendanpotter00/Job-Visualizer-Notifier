The plan

A new account answers three short questions, one on each screen.

It sits with 1/3, above the bar, on every step. A screen reader hears Question 1 of 3. The bar is hidden from it. Back is disabled on question 1. Continue never waits for an answer. An empty step means any.

A tap on Software Engineering selects it and shows 17 specialties.

Shown only when sweSubcategoriesEnabled is on. Business Ops is only Business Ops. Risk GOV.UK found that a checkbox that shows more checkboxes can confuse screen reader users. The specialties come right after it, in their own fieldset, and the label names the count. Test with VoiceOver and TalkBack.

Each question is optional, and Back keeps the earlier answers.

The location question shows six common places above a search.

Each new step moves focus to its question.

Nothing shows behind the questions. On a desktop the content is one 560px column. The flag is one-way.

The questions show once for each account and never again.

A nullable column on users holds the stamp; NULL means ask.

The migration stamps all 368 accounts that exist today.

A default of now() stamps every new account too.

The gate reads the server stamp, not the sign-in event.
